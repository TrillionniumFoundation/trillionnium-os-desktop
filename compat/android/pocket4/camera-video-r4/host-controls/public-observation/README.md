## Public schema and qualification rules

`public-record.schema.json` has two distinct states. The pending state fixes
zero passed controls, all eight names in `not_run`, and every host/runtime/app
qualification flag to false. Its reconciliation fields cannot replace the
original launch or cancellation UNKNOWN with a successful terminal result.

The completed-host state is a future projection format, not an assertion that
execution occurred. Producing it requires reviewing the original private inputs
and receipts together:

1. Recheck every pinned source and tool identity. The exact runner must have
   executed `qualify-host` with canonical host support inputs, not inspection
   relocations or a replacement Session admission implementation.
2. Require the runner's exact eight control names in order, each passed, with
   no failed, skipped or not-run controls; no error/cleanup/resource exception;
   and a qualified host component result. Match every referenced receipt's
   actual bytes and hash, not merely a caller-provided success dictionary.
3. Check the normal probe, video and PCM decodes used the unchanged production
   decoder and real capture/reaper. The UID-1000/two-FD sample belongs to the
   fixed helper. Do not describe it as an in-process sample from every decoder.
   The seal negative must preserve bytes, size and seals while handling any
   real timestamp change without rebasing production identity. The expected
   finish-fault record must remain UNKNOWN after separate finite cleanup.
4. Require final ECHILD, restored own subreaper state, unchanged own FD set,
   removed synthetic inputs, source/tool stability and successful final idle
   checks. Require the same cgroup identity/limits before and after, actual
   memory/task peaks within their limits, zero memory/swap/task limit events
   and no swap use. Preserve an unavailable optional peak as null, never zero.
5. Require the stager's exit 0, `owned_host_job_complete=true`, no client timeout,
   exact manager constraint readback and observed retirement of its own unit.
   Independently require the authoritative H job to have exit 0, complete and
   durable output and confirmed terminal publication. Source inspection,
   valid JSON, unit absence or a successful child alone is insufficient.
6. Publish a new completed observation only for its exact bound execution.
   Preserve this UNKNOWN observation and its original receipts unchanged.
   Do not use a later unrelated job to relabel the original action.

Both states keep synthetic media and inert Session scope explicit. Main,
Android and physical audio action counts remain zero. Live Session admission,
whole runtime and whole-app business acceptance remain false. This host proof
cannot clear Camera video permission, actual CAMCORDER input, owned-URI,
display/recovery or real-device prerequisites.

The public allowlist omits host/user names, absolute paths, unit/cgroup names,
InvocationID, private PIDs, FD numbers, timestamps used for process identity,
raw argv, nonce, raw stdout/stderr and media. Completed resources expose only
numeric limits/peaks, zero event totals and reviewed constraint values. Raw
receipts are represented by their commitments; public hashes alone do not
permit an independent replay of private evidence.

Schema conformance checks structure and the public allowlist. It does not
execute the host controls or authenticate a supplied success claim. Every
qualified projection still requires the receipt checks above.
