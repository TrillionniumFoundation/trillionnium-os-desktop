## Action contract

These existing actions and their current-value predicate are unchanged. It then reads at most four fresh complete result trees within 12 seconds and requires two consecutive complete tree/window/foreground/accessibility observations to agree.

One additional click is allowed. Its own fresh inspection must contain exactly one original title resource with the pinned full UTF-8 text digest, and that title's current direct parent must be an enabled, visible, non-password clickable ViewGroup under the current result pager. The same current card must contain the exact uploader and duration digests. The title, uploader and duration were independently checked against the actual 178-node results tree. Only hashes, resource identifiers and this relationship are published; the private tree and its other content are excluded.

`video_navigation.py` provides a narrow temporary contract view around the unchanged `Session.sequence`. It replaces only selection of the card node, using this action's fresh inspection and actual foreground scope. All other contract methods delegate unchanged. The view is restored in `finally`. On successful click acknowledgement the flow performs three separately fresh read-only inspect/state sequences. These provide private evidence for the next playback step; the controller does not infer playback, sound or business success. A primary failure retains its cause and allows only one independent diagnostic read, with the original Session's independent after-state still required. Any failed read stops the list. No click or failed command is replayed.
